# RIFTBOUND — design baseline

## The promise
A compact four-player Bedrock survival season where alliances, rivalries, and exploration matter. Losing a fight must sting, but must not delete a player's entire progression.

## Design pillars
1. **Small, deliberate content set** — every item or mechanic must have a clear gameplay role.
2. **Recoverable loss** — death and PvP create a setback, never a total wipe.
3. **Shared world, player choice** — cooperation is useful; rivalry is possible but not forced.
4. **Phone-first performance** — avoid heavy particles, huge entity counts, and constant polling.

## Planned gameplay loop
Explore and gather → earn rare Rift resources → craft a small set of signature gear → contest objectives → recover and adapt after defeat.

## Scope control
No random pile of dozens of ores, weapons, mobs, or dimensions. Additions must reinforce the core loop and pass multiplayer testing.

## Release gate
A pack is not called playable/stable until it imports on Android Bedrock, activates cleanly, and is tested with four players in one world. Back up worlds before testing.
